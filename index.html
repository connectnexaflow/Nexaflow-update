Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What does Nexaflow do?

Nexaflow is a digital marketing agency in Lucknow. We offer social media management, content creation and editing, ad campaigns, core marketing and branding, web development, AI visibility (AEO and GEO), local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization. We also build appointment software and WhatsApp automation for clinics.

What does social media management include?

We plan content calendars, create and edit reels, carousels and graphics, post across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, reply to comments and DMs, and send a monthly performance report.

What is AI visibility (AEO and GEO)?

AI visibility means getting your brand recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content to be quoted as the answer. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) builds the brand signals AI models use to describe you.

How long does AI visibility take?

8 out of 10 Nexaflow clients became visible in AI answers within 3 months. Timelines depend on your industry and competition, and we start with a free AI visibility audit.

Which ad platforms do you run?

We run Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Ads, Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads, focused on lead generation. Every campaign has tracking, so you see leads and cost per lead, not just clicks.

How long does it take to rank on Google Maps?

Many clients see movement within a few weeks of Google Business Profile optimization and local SEO. Results depend on niche, city and competition, and we do not make blanket ranking guarantees.

What is your clinic appointment software and WhatsApp automation?

Our appointment software handles online booking, reminders and schedules. Our WhatsApp automation sends instant replies, confirmations and follow-ups so no patient enquiry is missed. You can book a demo for both.

How much do Nexaflow's services cost?

Google Business Profile setup starts from ₹499. Social media, ad campaigns, AI visibility, web development and software are priced by scope. Contact us for a custom quote, and start with a free audit with no commitment.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Lucknow, Kanpur, Unnao, Barabanki, Sitapur, Raebareli, Hardoi and Ayodhya, and work with businesses across India remotely.

Which industries do you work with?

Clinics and healthcare, startups and SaaS, coaching and education, restaurants and salons, real estate and retail, and other local businesses that want to be seen on social, Google and AI.
